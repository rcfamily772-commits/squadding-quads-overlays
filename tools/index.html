Field testing and limitations. Radio widgets target compatible colour-screen EdgeTX radios and still require physical hardware validation. Test bench telemetry and screenshots are illustrative or simulated. RF Doctor does not measure actual latency or confirmed failsafes; LiPo Butler does not estimate battery health. OSD HEX editing is experimental and restricted to a supported cosmetic heading: bench validation with props removed is essential. OBS overlays use demo data; live integrations are not enabled. Wallpaper v1.6 includes the original 32 artworks in the widget and previews them all on the website. Radio Toolbox direct storage installation is browser/device dependent and hardware testing is pending. LiPo Lab predictions are modelled estimates, not flight-tested results or safe flight timers. Range Lab transmission ratings and travel models are educational, not safe or legal flight distances. Pilot Garage stores profiles locally; export a JSON backup before clearing browser storage. Rate Lab v1.1 is a work-in-progress preview with a startup warning and persistent red banner; do not use its settings until released.
