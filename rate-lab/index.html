05 / Understand the three settings

Centre sensitivity describes the slope near zero stick. Maximum rate is the requested speed at full deflection (subject to the rate cap). Expo reshapes the transition. Actual Rates separates these parameters; legacy Betaflight's parameters interact.

Research & limitations

Curve equations follow the Betaflight firmware implementations of applyActualRates and applyBetaflightRates, with the per-axis rate cap applied. The official default is 70 / 670 / 0 on all axes; other SQ presets are illustrative starting points, not verified pilot profiles or safety recommendations.
