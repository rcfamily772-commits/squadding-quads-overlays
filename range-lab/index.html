08 / Why a video link fails

Signal range vs protocol range

Radio waves take about 3.34 microseconds per kilometre one way. Bidirectional systems may encounter packet timing limits even with adequate RF strength. The DJI limits listed are independent estimates, not official specifications. HDZero broadcasts video without the same bidirectional video timing constraint; no verified universal maximum is not the same as infinite range.

Why a forest or bando can be harder than open air

Obstructions absorb and reflect radio waves; aircraft orientation, polarisation mismatch and competing transmitters can degrade the link. A short flight behind reinforced concrete can lose video well before a long clear line-of-sight flight. No fixed distance or penetration multiplier is supported here.

What happens if the radio link fails?

Video and control are different links. A clear video feed does not prove reliable control. Understand your aircraft's failsafe configuration and test it safely with propellers removed. GPS Rescue, where fitted, is not a guarantee of recovery.

Research methodology, limits and references

Research snapshot: 25 November 2026. v1.1 adds P1, Ascent, analogue, radio-control research and receiver/firmware evidence. Official ratings are preferred for published distances; independent reviews are used only for reported hard caps. Manufacturer tests are not independent validation. HDZero V1's test is recorded separately; the general HDZero technology page reports up to 7.3 km line-of-sight without a model-specific regional rating. There are no SQ-measured long-distance results in this version.

Endurance radius = usable hours / (1 / outbound ground speed + 1 / return ground speed). Usable hours = minutes / 60 × (1 − reserve/100). Free-space path loss = 32.4478 + 20 log10(distance in km) + 20 log10(frequency in MHz). Equivalent distance fraction = 10^(−extra dB/20).
