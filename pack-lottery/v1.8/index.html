Install

  1. Back up your SD card. Extract the ZIP and merge WIDGETS/SQLotto into WIDGETS, replacing main.lua.
  2. Restart, select SQLotto and open full screen. Draw or Deal before take-off; Landed after landing. Deck cycles six levels, Stats opens Passport; Next cycles Passport, Vault and Achievements.
  3. Widget settings: Motion on by default, SFX and Haptic off. Enable only if supported and useful.

Colour screens 320 × 240 and larger. Save data uses album-a.txt and album-b.txt in the widget folder; back up both. Radio progress is separate from browser progress and starts at v1.8. If saving fails, the card shows NOT SAVED. No control or model settings are changed. Physical radio and device audio/haptic checks remain open. Previous v1.7.
