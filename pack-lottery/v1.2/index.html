Install or upgrade

  1. Download and extract the v1.2 ZIP.
  2. Copy WIDGETS/SQLotto/ into the existing WIDGETS directory on your radio's SD card. If upgrading, replace the old main.lua; preserve your other widgets.
  3. Restart the radio and choose SQLotto in Screen Settings → Setup widgets.
  4. Open the widget full screen. Use Level to cycle through all five levels, then Draw. After landing, use Landed it, Try again or Redraw.

Colour-screen EdgeTX radios, 320 × 240 or larger. Radio tallies reset on reload; the browser companion saves its session locally. The new decks have matching Lua and browser messages; physical radio testing remains open. Complete your safety checks and fly only where safe and legal.
