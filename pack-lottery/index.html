Get it on your radio

  1. Unzip the download. Copy SQLotto into the SD card’s existing WIDGETS folder. Check for /WIDGETS/SQLotto/main.lua.
  2. Restart the radio. Open Screen Settings → Setup widgets, select a zone and choose SQLotto.
  3. Double-tap the tile, or select it, press Enter and choose Full screen. Use touch or the navigation keys and Enter to choose Level, then Draw.

After landing

Landed it scores the card. Try again keeps it for another attempt. Redraw deals the next one. Hold Return to leave full screen. Your tally resets when you reload the widget or restart the radio.

Field preview 1.7
