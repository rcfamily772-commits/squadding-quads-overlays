Installation & compatibility
  1. Back up your SD card. Choose your exact screen size and export a ZIP.
  2. Extract into the SD root, merging IMAGES and WIDGETS. Replace main.lua and remove any stale main.luac. Keep last.txt and existing splash.
  3. Restart, assign SQWall to a colour-screen widget zone, and open full screen for Prev, Lucky, Next and Clean.

Monochrome radios use static artwork only. The widget displays artwork in its assigned zone, not the global radio theme. Physical-radio testing of v1.4 is pending. Existing gallery PNGs remain on the radio when merging.
