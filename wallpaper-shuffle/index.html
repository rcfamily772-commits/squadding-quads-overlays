Original v1.0 installation

  1. Unzip the kit. Copy SQWall into your SD card’s existing WIDGETS folder and SQWALL into IMAGES.
  2. Restart, open Screen Settings → Setup widgets and choose SQWall. A single-zone home page gives the artwork the most room.
  3. Open the widget full screen to shuffle by touch or Enter. Hold Return to show just the artwork.

Bring your own wallpapers (v1.0)

Drop landscape PNGs into /IMAGES/SQWALL/ and reload the widget. The included images are 480 × 272. The browser test bench lets you try your own PNGs at four radio screen sizes before copying anything to your SD card.
