Installation & compatibility
  1. Back up your SD card. Download the ZIP for your exact screen size.
  2. Extract into the SD root, merging WIDGETS. Replace main.lua and remove any stale main.luac. Keep last.txt and existing splash.
  3. Restart, assign SQWall to a colour-screen widget zone, and open full screen for Prev, Lucky, Next and Clean.

Monochrome radios use static artwork only. The widget displays artwork in its assigned zone, not the global radio theme. Physical-radio testing of v1.5 is pending. The built-in artwork lives inside the widget; existing custom gallery PNGs remain supported.
