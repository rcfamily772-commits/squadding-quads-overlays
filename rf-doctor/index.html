Install and record

  1. Extract the ZIP. Merge WIDGETS/SQRF into the SD card WIDGETS directory; restart the radio.
  2. With your receiver connected, discover telemetry sensors in the radio model's Telemetry menu. Expected names: RQly/Rqly, 1RSS, 2RSS, RSNR, TPWR, RFMD, TQly.
  3. Assign SQRF to a colour widget zone, then open full screen. Select Record before take-off and Stop / save after landing. Graph cycles the chart sensor; View cycles dashboard, report and sensor status.
  4. Copy the CSV from /WIDGETS/SQRF/RF-*.csv and open the browser analyser. Recordings are capped at one hour; buffered rows are flushed every five samples or on Stop.

Scope and limitations

Targets colour-screen EdgeTX 2.8+ at 320 × 240 or larger. ELRS and Crossfire-style named telemetry sensors are the initial target; other protocols or renamed sensors may show missing fields. Samples are approximately once per second, including while hidden when EdgeTX schedules the background callback. Missing or stale telemetry is blank, not zero. RX gaps mean missing receiver-side readings, not measured failsafes. RFMD is a raw mode code, not a packet rate in Hz. The Low LQ setting is a report threshold, not a safety alarm. No actual latency, control-link range, failsafe events or packet-by-packet loss are measured. No radio settings are modified. Hardware validation is pending.

Never rely on this diagnostic widget as a substitute for normal radio warnings, safe flying or range checks. Use one recording instance per model and stop the recording before switching model or powering off. SD writes are best-effort and an interrupted session may lose up to the buffered rows.
